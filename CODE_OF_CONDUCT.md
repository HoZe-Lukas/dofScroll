# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(CC BY-SA 4.0), in a short form.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and respectful. Assume good intent and ask when something is unclear.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code and ideas, not about people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people who write in a language that is not their own.
  The project language is English; see `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
- Say clearly what you did and did not test. Do not present guesses as facts.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information (for example addresses, hostnames or device
  identifiers) without permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, the wiki and any other space where
someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem privately through GitHub: open
<https://github.com/HoZe-Lukas/dofScroll/security/advisories/new> (**Report a vulnerability**,
write "conduct report" in the title) or contact the maintainer **@HoZe-Lukas** through GitHub.
There is no e-mail address for reports. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who violate
this code. Serious or repeated violations lead to a permanent ban.
